# Reported results and limitations

The fixed 200-source image comparison is retrospective: the sources had been inspected during earlier development. It is not independent confirmation.

| Subset | Sources | PSNR (dB) | SSIM | LPIPS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Complex in-domain | 120 | 30.585 | 0.8416 | 0.1175 |
| Spline trajectory OOD | 40 | 28.609 | 0.8151 | 0.1530 |
| Simple kernels | 40 | 35.140 | 0.9329 | 0.0622 |

These are the complete selected SKF-Diff configuration, not isolated component gains. Spectral evidence, residual parameterization and extra training exposure changed together. Matched component ablations remain incomplete. A same-domain Concertormer control is stronger on complex blur (32.788 dB); its complete results are retained in the supplement. Same-domain DiffIR improves strongly over released S2, showing the importance of adaptation, but does not prove a physical-PSF innovation. Some difficult sources retain large errors, and the quantitative metrics do not establish removal of ringing. Real-image validation remains open.

Example panels were chosen retrospectively for illustration. Their detail crops use identical coordinates across methods and are not an additional statistical comparison. No new result is generated by the synthetic code tests.
